through the Service (for example, if you open a document stored in Google Drive).

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide directly
- **Account information:** name, email address, password (stored only as a salted cryptographic
  hash — we never store or have access to your plaintext password), and, if provided, a firm name.
- **Case and client information you enter:** case names, client names, and any descriptions or
  billing narratives you type.
- **Billing details you enter:** your billable rate.

### 2.2 Activity data (manual entry or automatic capture)
Whether you enter an activity manually in the web app or it is captured automatically by the
desktop agent, we record:
- The activity **type** (document, email, or call)
- **Start time, end time, and duration**
- A **title/label** for the activity — specifically: a document file name, an email subject line
  and sender/recipient address, or a contact name and phone number, depending on the activity type
- Whether the activity is billable, and any notes or narrative you add
- Which case (if any) the activity is assigned to

**What we do not collect, ever, through automatic tracking:** the body or content of emails, the
content of documents or files, call audio or transcripts, keystrokes, clipboard contents, or
screenshots. The desktop agent works by reading only the **title bar** of your active window (the
same text visible in your Windows taskbar) — it has no access to and does not read what's inside
that window. We are not able to reconstruct email or document content from the data we collect.

One honest caveat: because window/tab titles are generated by the applications and websites you
use, they can occasionally include more than a file name (for example, a browser tab title that
echoes a search query). We filter automatic tracking to a defined set of work-related applications
and websites (see §2.3) specifically to limit this, but we cannot guarantee that a title captured
from a third-party application never contains incidental content.

### 2.3 Desktop agent scope and controls
The optional desktop agent only tracks activity in a defined allowlist of applications (e.g.
Microsoft Word/Excel/PowerPoint, Adobe Acrobat, Outlook, Zoom/Teams) and a defined allowlist of
work-related websites (e.g. Gmail, Outlook Web, legal research and document-storage sites). It
explicitly excludes common non-work sites (social media, video/music streaming, etc.) from tracking
entirely. You can turn tracking off at any time from the "Privacy & Controls" page in the web app;
the desktop agent checks for this setting roughly every 15 seconds and stops logging new activity
once it picks up the change. You can also pause or resume tracking directly from the agent's system
tray menu.

The agent authenticates to our backend using a per-user API key (not your password), which is
stored locally on your device. You can revoke this key at any time from your Profile page, which
immediately prevents that installation of the agent from submitting further activity.

### 2.4 Information collected automatically by our servers
Standard web/API request metadata (e.g. timestamps, IP address at time of request) may be logged by
our hosting infrastructure for security and reliability purposes. The LawTrack website itself does
not use cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party trackers.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the Service (recording, assigning, and reporting on billable time)
- Authenticate you and secure your account
- Generate reports and invoices you request
- Auto-assign activities to cases based on rules you configure
- Diagnose technical problems and maintain the security of the Service
- Communicate with you about your account or the Service

We do **not** use your data to train third-party AI models, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account is active, so that you can
continue to use timesheet and reporting features.

If you delete your account (available from your Profile page), we immediately and permanently
delete your case data, activity records, auto-assignment rules, and any agent API keys from our
live database. Your user account record is also deleted at that time.

## 6. Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your information:
- Passwords are never stored in plaintext — they are hashed using bcrypt-based hashing before storage.
- Desktop agent API keys are never stored in plaintext on our servers — only a cryptographic hash
  is stored, with a short prefix used for lookup.
- Data in transit between your browser or desktop agent and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
- Access to production data is limited to what is necessary to operate the Service.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
